About
Who I am

I'm a Lecturer and AI Engineer at Kapadokya University, teaching courses from AI Literacy to Deep Learning applications to over 900 students each semester. My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and dentistry — using YOLO-based models to detect dental anatomy and identify implant brands from radiographic images.

Before joining academia, I spent over a year at CERN in Geneva as a Technical Student, developing embedded systems and data pipelines for the CMS Detector temperature monitoring project — selected from thousands of applicants.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering and a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (double major, ranked 2nd in both), and an M.Sc. in Electrical & Computer Engineering (GPA: 3.96) — all fully funded. Currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering at Eskişehir Technical University.
