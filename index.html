About
Who I am

I'm a Lecturer and AI Engineer at Kapadokya University, where I teach courses ranging from AI Literacy to Deep Learning applications. My research sits at the intersection of artificial intelligence and dentistry — using YOLO-based computer vision models to detect dental anatomy and identify implant brands from radiographic images.

Before joining academia, I spent over a year at CERN in Geneva as a Technical Student, developing embedded systems and data pipelines for the CMS Detector temperature monitoring project.

I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering, a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering (double major, both ranked 2nd in class), and an M.Sc. in Electrical & Computer Engineering — all fully funded. I am currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering at Eskişehir Technical University.

I'm passionate about bridging cutting-edge AI research with real clinical impact, and building tools that make AI more accessible for educators and practitioners alike.
